Our Story

Rooted in tradition, strung by hand

Mala Tree began as a simple idea: spiritual jewelry should feel personal, not mass-produced. Founded by Simerbersingh Bedi in Patiala, every piece that leaves our hands — a single bracelet or a full 108-bead mala — is selected, cleaned and strung individually, the same way it has been done for generations.
